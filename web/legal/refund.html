Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: April 12, 2026  |  RBI & Consumer Protection Act Compliant
Quick Summary: The ₹99 Blue Tick verification fee is refundable only if your verification application is rejected by our editorial team or if there is a technical/duplicate payment error. Approved verifications are non-refundable. Please read the full policy below.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Refund and Cancellation Policy governs all payments made on the NewsXpressLive platform operated by SoftDigi Technologies Pvt. Ltd., including Blue Tick verification fees processed via Razorpay. This Policy is governed by the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, RBI guidelines on payment aggregators, and IT Act, 2000.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Verification fee where the Blue Tick has been approved and activated.
  • Fee paid for an Early Bird slot (₹0 — no charge applicable).
  • Situations where the applicant provided false or misleading documents.
  • Voluntarily requested account deletions after approval.
  • Blue Tick revoked due to content moderation strikes (3rd strike).
  • Situations where the applicant changed their mind after submission without rejection.
  • Processing fees charged by Razorpay (approximately 2% + GST) are not refundable by the Platform.

4. ✅ Conditions Eligible for Full Refund

You are entitled to a full refund of the base fee (₹99 for Reporter / ₹299 for Agency) in the following cases:
  1. Verification Rejected by Editorial Team: If your Blue Tick application is rejected after document review, a full refund of the base fee (excluding GST and Razorpay gateway charges) will be initiated automatically within 5–7 business days.
  2. Duplicate/Double Payment: If you are charged twice for the same application due to a technical error, the duplicate payment will be refunded within 3 business days.
  3. Payment Debited but Order Not Created: If your payment was deducted but the application was not registered on our system (gateway failure), a full refund will be issued within 5 business days upon verification.
  4. Payment Made by Mistake by Wrong User: If a registered user accidentally paid for another account, the fee will be refunded within 7 business days after investigation.
  5. System Error/Tech Failure: If a documented system failure on our side caused incorrect billing, the full amount will be refunded.

5. Partial Refund — Early Bird Discount Users

Special Case: If a Reporter who claimed an Early Bird slot (₹0) is later asked to upgrade to a paid plan due to slot limit exhaustion or policy changes, and if the paid application is subsequently rejected, the full paid amount shall be refunded per Section 4 conditions.

Upon rejection of a verification application, our system automatically initiates a Razorpay refund. No manual action is needed from the applicant. The refund reference ID will be sent to your registered email.

6.2 Manual Refund Request

For cases other than automatic rejection (e.g., duplicate payment), submit your refund request to:

  • Email: refunds@newsxpresslive.com
  • Subject: "Refund Request — [Your Registered Mobile Number]"
  • Include: Payment transaction ID (from Razorpay), date of payment, registered email.

7. Cancellation Policy

  • You may cancel a verification application at any time before payment at no charge.
  • Once payment is made, cancellation is not possible during the review phase.
  • If you wish to withdraw your application after payment, please email support@newsxpresslive.com. Cancellation post-payment does not guarantee a refund unless the conditions in Section 4 are met.

8. Payment Disputes with Razorpay

If you believe an incorrect charge has been applied, you may also raise a dispute directly with Razorpay via their support portal at https://razorpay.com/support. We will cooperate fully with any chargeback investigations.

Important — Chargebacks: Initiating an unjustified chargeback with your bank for a valid approved Blue Tick may result in suspension of your account pending investigation. We reserve the right to pursue recovery of fraudulently reversed payments.

9. Consumer Rights (Consumer Protection Act, 2019)

You have the right to:

  • File a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline: 1800-11-4000
  • File a complaint on the Consumer App / portal: consumerhelpline.gov.in
  • Approach the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (CDRC).

10. RBI and IT Act Compliance Note

  • All payment transactions comply with RBI's Guidelines on Regulation of Payment Aggregators and Payment Gateways (March 2020).
  • Razorpay holds a valid authorisation from RBI as a payment aggregator.
  • Transaction records are maintained for 5 years as required by RBI.
  • PCI-DSS compliance ensured; no raw card data is stored on our servers.
  • Refund processing complies with NPCI's guidelines for UPI refunds and card network rules.
