5. Consent (DPDP Act, 2023 — Section 6)

We obtain your free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent before processing your personal data. Consent is recorded at the time of registration. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at dpo@newsxpresslive.com.

Note: Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Withdrawal may result in inability to access certain features (e.g., Blue Tick requires KYC).

9. Data Security Measures

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit; AES-256 at rest for sensitive fields.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for internal systems.
  • Database credentials stored in server environment variables, never in code.
  • No credit card or card data stored; Razorpay handles PCI-DSS compliance.
  • Redis and MySQL on private networks with firewall rules.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Incident response plan: data breaches notified to users within 72 hours as required under DPDP Act, 2023.

10. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act, 2023)

✅ Right to Access (S.11) Request a summary of personal data we process and the purposes.
✏️ Right to Correction (S.12) Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
🗑️ Right to Erasure (S.12) Request deletion of personal data no longer necessary, subject to legal retention requirements.
📋 Right to Grievance Redressal (S.13) Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer; response within 30 days.
🚫 Right to Withdraw Consent (S.6) Withdraw consent at any time. May affect feature availability.
🏛️ Right to Complain to DPBI Escalate unresolved grievances to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any right, email dpo@newsxpresslive.com with subject line "DPDP Request — [Right Type]". We will respond within 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover that a minor has registered, we will immediately delete their account and associated data.

12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies for session management, analytics (Firebase Analytics), and personalisation. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function correctly. The app uses Firebase Analytics for crash reporting and usage analytics; you may opt out via app settings.
